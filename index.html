About Me

Hello! I’m Akanksha, a recent graduate in Aerospace Engineering who found a compelling career path in Artificial Intelligence. My curiosity for algorithms began in my second year, and quickly grew into exploring how they can automate processes, uncover insights, and support decision making.

During my internship semester, I worked as a Computer Vision Research Trainee at CSIR-CSIO for 6 months, where I explored deep learning architectures - especially transformers and attention mechanisms - and co-authored a research paper (currently under review).

Beyond research, I am a self-driven learner who enjoys building end-to-end ML pipelines, predictive models, and in-depth EDA through personal projects. I thrive in dynamic environments where curiosity, problem-solving, and collaboration intersect.

As I grow, my goal is to leverage machine learning, statistical modeling, and deep learning to create meaningful impact - in both industry and real-world applications.

Always open to connecting, collaborating, and learning something new!
